## Verdict

This iteration fixed what it said it fixed, with one exception that shows up on screen. The header now reads "0 AI calls" on both keyless runs. Directory pages are gone from the lineup. The role criteria card lists only the three must-haves and reads "Location compatible with Prague". The purge deletes every table and the Screen 1 line now says "deleted after 7 days". A plain curl to the start route gets 403.

The exception is the interview questions. Buryan's brief asks "Is the Instagram account dcyoaufaoyx yours?" and "Is the Instagram account dbvybjfptwj yours?". Those are Instagram post codes, not accounts. `canonicalProfile` in `src/recipe/seams/resolve.ts` excludes `/p/`, then the generic fallback takes the last path segment as the handle. I reproduced it: `instagram.com/p/DCYoauFAOYx/` gives handle `dcyoaufaoyx`, and `/reels/`, `/tv/`, `/stories/` and `/popular/` give handles "reels", "tv", "stories" and "popular". A judge reads that first in the most actionable card on the page.

The second new problem is the lineup on a common name. Pokorný's 12 slots are 6 LinkedIn and 6 GitHub. Four GitHub rows are Martin, Daniel, Robin and Alex Pokorný, who share only the surname. Because platforms rank before the 12 cap, these namesakes crowd out Instagram, X and Facebook. The Instagram hit that mentioned Liberec in 003 is no longer in the lineup.
