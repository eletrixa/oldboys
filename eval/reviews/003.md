## Verdict

The structural identity bug is fixed for real. SERP hits start unverified, and `applySourceIdentity` in `src/adapters/d1.ts` re-marks them merged only when their profile key matches a candidate the user confirmed. Extract and the evidence list filter on that identity alone. Buryan's "From profiles you confirmed" now holds only his own LinkedIn: CMO Groupon, 11 teams, Zajímám se. The namesakes sit in a collapsed "Mentions of the name, not confirmed (26)" box. That is the first brief a judge could trust.

The hole has moved to the lineup. Directory and search pages such as "30+ profilů „Lukáš Pokorný“" and "100+ \"Lukas Pokorny\" profiles" become LinkedIn candidates. Two of Pokorný's three questions were spent on them. A "Yes" on one turns a snippet that lists several different people into confirmed evidence. With the key set, that snippet goes to extract as trusted material. This is now the single worst thing a jury can trigger, and a one-line noise rule closes it.

Two claimed fixes are not real or not complete. The AI call counter still says "2 AI calls" on both keyless runs. And `/api/start` attaches `RUN_TOKEN` to any anonymous POST, so the start gate is now weaker than the spoofable header in 002, not stronger.

## Top 10 fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Directory and search pages are never candidates.** In `src/recipe/seams/resolve.ts`, add LinkedIn `/pub/dir/`, `/search/` and any `linkedin.com` URL without `/in/` or `/company/` to `isNoise`. Do the same for Facebook `/public/` and title patterns like "profilů" or "profiles". Add a test with `https://cz.linkedin.com/pub/dir/Lukáš/Pokorný` asserting it never becomes a draft.
2. **Make the AI call count true.** In `src/workflow/research-run.ts`, write `calls` on the `role_questions` row and on the resolve row from `out.calls`. Have `roleQuestions` in `src/recipe/seams/role.ts` return `calls`. In `src/domain/run-cost.ts`, default a missing `calls` to 0. Add a test: a keyless run projects 0 AI calls.
3. **Close the open start route.** In `src/app/api/start/route.ts`, require a Turnstile token or a short-lived signed nonce issued by the page render. Add a per-IP limit of a few runs per hour before attaching `RUN_TOKEN`.
4. **Replace the research-prompt card.** In `src/app/runs/[id]/parts.tsx` and `src/recipe/seams/synthesize.ts`, show only role criteria in the degraded brief. Title it "Role criteria (not checked, AI unavailable)". Drop base prompts that mention "the subject" and "the anchor". In `src/recipe/seams/role.ts`, build the location criterion from the anchor city, not the whole role string.
5. **Real interview questions.** In `synthesize.ts`, ask identity questions only for social profiles with a real handle, at most two. Add two or three role questions from a template, such as "Walk us through your most recent Senior Data Engineer work and the scale of the data you owned." Never ask about generic web pages.
6. **Canonicalise Facebook and YouTube.** In `canonicalProfile`, collapse `m.`, `web.` and `www.facebook.com` and `profile.php?id=` onto one key. Map YouTube `/@x` and `/channel/x` to a handle and give `watch?v=` a null handle. Label Facebook as "Facebook" in `platformOf` in `src/recipe/sources/types.ts`.
7. **Make the privacy promise true.** In `src/workflow/purge.ts`, also delete or blank `brief_json`, candidate snippets, claims and ledger `ref_json` for expired runs. In `src/app/start-form.tsx`, say "deleted after 7 days".
8. **Spend the three questions well.** In `src/app/runs/[id]/state.ts` or the lineup picker, ask the best-scoring candidate per platform, and prefer drafts that mention the anchor. Pokorný's Instagram "mentions Liberec" was never asked.
9. **Gaps that match the lineup.** Add a Facebook gap such as "Facebook: not searched, public profiles need a login" in `src/recipe/goals/hiring.ts`. Change the `talks_serp` empty text to "talk hits found, none confirmed" when unverified hits exist.
10. **Finish the trust labels.** Rewrite the degraded notice in `parts.tsx` as "AI is off for this run, so this brief shows sources only." Add a MOCK label in `src/app/runs/[id]/run-view.tsx` when `CALL_PROVIDER=mock`. Raise a ranked contradiction when two IČOs or two current employers are merged.

- Fix 1 lands with a test. A keyed Pokorný run asks about real profiles only, and no confirmed section quotes a directory page.
- Fixes 2 and 3 land. The header count matches real model calls, and curl cannot start a run.
- A keyed run produces FACT claims with verbatim quotes from merged sources only, labels INFERENCE, and shows at least one contradiction by rank.
- The top of the brief answers the hiring question in 10 seconds: current role, tenure, evidence per role criterion, and three to five real interview questions (fixes 4 and 5).
- The E3 goal delta is shown live: hiring and due-diligence on the same subject differ by at least 50% of claims.
- End to end stays at or under 4 minutes with Opus on both demo subjects.
- The privacy line matches the purge (fix 7).

Without fix 1, setting the key lets one "Yes" on a directory page feed several strangers' job titles to extract as confirmed material.
