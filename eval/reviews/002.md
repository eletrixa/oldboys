## Verdict

Iteration 002 fixed the dead end. A run now always ends in a brief, the lineup asks about LinkedIn first, failures are explained, and both runs finished in about 2 minutes. The fallback no longer merges on a city substring, and name-search collectors are quarantined as "also found". That is real progress. But the core identity promise is still broken, just one layer up. Every Google SERP hit is treated as confirmed, by design, in both `extract.ts` and `synthesize.ts`. So the degraded brief opens with "Below is everything we confirmed, with sources" and then lists a World of Tanks player, St Buryan Academy, a UK company called BURYAN LIMITED and "The St Buryan Sessions" for Buryan. For Pokorný it lists a Slovan Liberec footballer, a tennis player, a Junior ML engineer, a CzechTourism director and a procurement MBA as one person. Once the key is set, those same SERP hits are what extract reads, so the brief will state facts about namesakes with real citations. That is the single worst thing the jury can find, and it is now a one-line filter away. Note that review 001 itself recommended "SERP plus merged" for extract. That advice was wrong, and this review corrects it.

## By criterion

**1. Screen 1 (4.0, no change).** The token is out of the bundle and the dev footer is gone. The replacement is weak. `src/app/api/runs/route.ts` skips the bearer check when the request carries the header `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`. Browsers set that header, but curl can send any header, so the bearer token no longer protects anything. Exposure is capped at 20 runs per hour at $0.50 each, about $240 a day. The privacy line and error mapping from 001 are unchanged.

**2. Screen 2 (3.5, +1.0).**
- Good: LinkedIn is asked first for Buryan, with the platform mark and the "@josef-buryan" handle. Row 1 reads "Searching public sources" until hits arrive. The bar moves with real steps. "Keep this tab open" replaces the false promise. Answers stop after sending.
- Every lineup row shows "fallback: name match only" or "fallback: anchor found in text, please confirm". The wireframe shows human reasons such as "Links to the same LinkedIn profile". "fallback:" is internal jargon.
- On a degraded run all five progress rows get a green tick, including "Double-checking facts against each other". Nothing was checked.
- The header says "5 AI calls" on a run where no model call succeeded. `src/domain/run-cost.ts` counts ledger rows of kind llm, not model calls.
- The brief is a 5,500 to 5,700 pixel wall. Nine question cards repeat the identical sentence "AI summary unavailable: no verified claims". The evidence list is unranked and 40 items long.
- Step ids leak into the brief: "linkedin_profile: not searched", "Confirm with the candidate (huggingface_profile)".

**3. Breadth (3.5, +0.5).**
- Good: skips now say "not searched: no confirmed handle or id to look up". Free REST fetches no longer exhaust the budget. Parallel batches cut the run to about 2 minutes, which leaves room for Opus.
- New bug: `resolve.ts` takes the first 12 sources before ranking by platform. Pokorný's SERP has at least five LinkedIn hits, but none made the lineup, so the LinkedIn collector logged "not searched". For a Senior Data Engineer hiring brief, missing LinkedIn is the most expensive gap possible.
- The "Not searched (and why)" list also contains searched-and-empty gaps, for example "github_profile: no public GitHub profile found" for Buryan.
- Facebook is still not collected.

**4. Identity (2.5, +1.0).**
- Good: no fallback merge without a hard link, quarantined name-search collectors with their own "Also found, not confirmed (19)" box, extract excludes unverified sources, and a tested cross-link rule.
- Broken: `confirmed()` in `synthesize.ts` and the source filter in `extract.ts` both accept `s.actor === SERP_ACTOR` unconditionally. The SERP query is the name plus a city, so it returns every namesake. The degraded brief presents them as confirmed. With the key set, they are fed to extract as trusted material.
- Rejected profiles are excluded by exact URL match against the canonical profile URL. A raw SERP URL such as a LinkedIn post, a query string variant or a missing "www" does not match. So a profile the user said "No" to can still reach extract through its SERP hit. Use `pageKey` or `canonicalProfile` on both sides.
- Candidate discovery is capped at the first 12 sources, so the right profile can be absent from the lineup entirely.

**5. Honesty and safety (3.0, +0.5).** Degrade-not-fail, truthful skip reasons, the "also found" label and the removed notification promise are all real gains. These remain or are new:
- "Everything we confirmed" heads a list of unconfirmed namesake pages.
- Green ticks on steps that did not run.
- An AI call count when no AI ran.
- "Not searched" covers things that were searched.
- The deletion promise has no purge behind it, and there are no CACHED or MOCK labels.
- The FACT gate and protected-category filter are intact. No person scoring.

**6. Judge value (2.5, +1.0).** A judge now gets something. Buryan's brief has a correct LinkedIn section (CMO Groupon, Vilgain, Notino) and around 40 linked mentions, in about 2 minutes. But the useful part has to be dug out of a wall of web snippets with namesakes mixed in. Pokorný's brief is mostly about other people. A jury that types a common Czech name will see that inside a minute. The degraded interview questions are research prompts, not interview questions. "Ask about: Which sources disagree with each other?" is not something a hiring manager can ask a candidate.

## Top 10 fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Stop treating SERP hits as confirmed.** In `src/recipe/seams/synthesize.ts` (`confirmed`) and `src/recipe/seams/extract.ts`, drop `|| s.actor === SERP_ACTOR`. A SERP source counts only when its `pageKey` equals the `pageKey` of a merged candidate's profile URL. After the lineup answer, have the Workflow set `identity = 'merged'` on those sources. Everything else goes to "also found". Add a test with the Pokorný footballer SERP hit, asserting it is not in the evidence and not in the extract prompt.
2. **Compare rejected profiles by page identity, not raw URL.** In `extract.ts` and `synthesize.ts`, build the rejected set from `pageKey(canonicalProfile(url).url)` and test sources the same way. A LinkedIn post or a query-string variant of a rejected profile must stay out.
3. **Rank before you cap.** In `src/recipe/seams/resolve.ts`, sort sources by `PROFILE_PLATFORMS` value (LinkedIn, GitHub, X first) before `.slice(0, 12)`, or cap per platform. Add a test where the LinkedIn hit is source 15 and must still become a candidate.
4. **Make the degraded brief readable.** In `src/app/runs/[id]/parts.tsx`, when `degraded` is set, show one notice and hide the per-question cards. Put merged profile evidence first, under the profile name. Retitle "Everything we confirmed" to "From profiles you confirmed", and move SERP hits into "Mentions of the name, not confirmed". Show 10 rows by default with "show more".
5. **Truthful progress and counts.** In `src/app/runs/[id]/state.ts`, mark rows 3 and 4 as skipped, with a grey dash rather than a tick, when the brief is degraded. In `src/domain/run-cost.ts`, count model calls from `ref.calls` rather than ledger rows, so a run without a key shows "0 AI calls".
6. **Split the gap lists and use human labels.** In `synthesize.ts`, put only "not searched:" gaps into `not_searched` and add a `searched_empty` list. In `parts.tsx`, render "Searched, nothing found" and "Not searched, and why" separately, with labels like "LinkedIn profile" in place of step ids such as `linkedin_profile`.
7. **Real interview questions in degraded mode.** In `synthesize.ts`, drop "Ask about: <research question>" and "Confirm with the candidate (<step id>)". Generate questions from what is actually open, using templates. For example, "We found a LinkedIn profile linkedin.com/in/x. Is it yours?" for each possibly-same-as profile. For each role criterion with no evidence, ask "Can you walk us through your <role> work at <current employer>?".
8. **Plain-word match reasons.** In `parts.tsx` (or at the source in `resolve.ts` `fallbackScores`), map the reasons to "Same name, mentions Liberec", "Same name only" and "Links to a profile you confirmed". Never show the word "fallback".
9. **Close the auth hole and the old trust gaps.** In `src/app/api/runs/route.ts`, do not trust `Sec-Fetch-Site`. Use a server action or a Turnstile check that attaches `RUN_TOKEN` server-side, and lower the anonymous cap. In `src/app/start-form.tsx`, map 400, 401 and 503 to distinct messages and change the copy to "deleted after 30 days". Back that copy with a `scheduled` purge in `src/worker.ts`. Add CACHED and MOCK labels in `run-view.tsx`. Delete `lineup-form.tsx` and `report/page.tsx`.
10. **Budget race in parallel batches.** In `src/workflow/research-run.ts`, all five steps in a batch read the same `ctx.spent`, so paid calls can exceed the cap of 16 and the $0.50 limit by up to four calls. Before `flush`, compute the remaining paid calls and give each step an allowance, or keep paid actor steps sequential and parallelise only free REST steps. Add a test.

## What moves this to 4.5 once the key is set

- Fixes 1 to 3 land, with tests. A keyed run on Pokorný, Liberec shows a LinkedIn candidate in the lineup. The footballer appears only under "also found". Not one claim cites a namesake page.
- A keyed run produces FACT claims with verbatim quotes, and the cited source is identity-merged in every case. INFERENCE claims are labeled. At least one contradiction is shown by rank, for example two IČOs or two current employers, rather than silently merged.
- The top of the brief answers the hiring question in under 10 seconds of reading: current role, tenure, evidence for each role criterion, and three to five real interview questions. The evidence wall sits below it, collapsed.
- The E3 goal-delta gate is shown live. Hiring and due-diligence runs on the same subject differ by at least 50% of their claims.
- With Opus in extract, verify and synthesize, end-to-end time stays at or under 4 minutes on both demo subjects, measured and shown in the header.
- Progress ticks, AI call counts and gap headings are all truthful. Fixes 5 and 6 are done.
- The trust items in fix 9 are done: purge job, CACHED and MOCK labels, no spoofable gate.

Without fixes 1 to 3, setting the key lowers the score. The model will turn namesake snippets into confident, cited claims.
