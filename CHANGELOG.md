Spend stays inside the run budget (about $0.05 per run, not counted as Apify runs); runbook `docs/ops/treg.md`.
- Research in progress, on the run page and in the "Briefs in progress" panel (plans/015): the page says how much is typically left as a range ("About 2 to 4 min left · 1 min 10 s in"), what each of the five steps is doing (the sources still being read, how long a finished step took, the typical time of the ones to come) and what was found so far (public mentions, accounts confirmed, accounts awaiting an answer), ticking once a second between polls; the panel row gets a progress bar, the same remaining-time line with what is read now, and "Answer now" when the run waits for the recruiter. The estimate comes from per-step typical times measured on production runs and is withdrawn ("Taking longer than usual, still working") once a step runs past 2.5x its typical time; a paused run shows no countdown (waiting time is not research time); the bar is time-weighted instead of jumping with the last finished step. The run page's header line points to the live estimate instead of repeating a fixed "usually N minutes". `GET /api/runs/:id/state` adds `progress` (five phases with typical times, open steps and known start / end times)
- Research goes deeper (plans/013): every hiring brief now checks SEC EDGAR full-text search (filings that name the person: Schedule 13D/13G, 8-K, 10-Q, Form 4, proxy statements), English and Czech Wikipedia, the Apple Podcasts index, and four more Google search packs (regulatory and stock-market pages, court / insolvency / enforcement pages, Czech and international business press, board and founder pages); the confirmed web pages are then read in full (up to 12, SEC filings first) and the paragraphs naming the person replace the search snippet, so facts quote the article or filing itself; extraction runs in batches so every source reaches the model; two new sections, "Filings and markets" and "Legal record". Run budget raised to $2.00 and 24 paid actor runs; the time promise reads "5 to 10 minutes"
- AI-drafted phone verification questions: when HR opens the call setup, `POST /api/runs/:id/calls/proposal` (session or bearer) asks Opus 5.5 once for 3–5 specific, open questions anchored on the confirmed research (must-haves with coverage, to-verify items and CV differences, weak or contradicted claims, profile risks / history / fit gaps / questions, plain "nothing found" gaps; never personality, unconfirmed namesake hits, URLs or tool notes), each with a `why` chip, a "listen for" note and one follow-up.
